# Intersect Brand Voice Alignment

When humanising text for the Intersect Developer Experience project, align the output with the Intersect brand voice defined in `BRAND.md`.

## Brand Characteristics

The rewritten text should feel:

- **Welcoming** — approachable, friendly, compassionate. Not cold, not corporate.
- **Inclusive** — mindful, supportive, never exclusionary.
- **Informed** — confident, clear, generous with knowledge.
- **Ambitious** — energetic and enthusiastic about breaking new ground.

## Language Rules

- Use **US English** and **AP style** for global communications.
- Use **contractions** where possible; we are welcoming, not overly formal.
- Use the **Oxford comma** to avoid ambiguity.
- Do **not** capitalize anything that is not a proper noun.
- Keep headlines and labels in **sentence case**.
- Never use exclusionary, culturally appropriated, ableist, or misgendering language.

## Tone Adjustments

- Replace stiff, defensive, or aloof phrasing with warm, direct statements.
- Preserve technical precision; do not dumb down concepts.
- Avoid AI padding like "It's important to note that", "In today's ever-evolving world", or "The future looks bright".
- Avoid hedging. State things confidently when the facts support it.
- Avoid sycophancy residue ("Great question!", "Absolutely!").
- When giving guidance, sound like a knowledgeable colleague, not a vendor.

## Preferred Language

Use words and phrases that reinforce unity, collaboration, and intersection:

- United, Unity, Collaboration, Intersection, Inclusivity
- Community, Diversity, Togetherness, Partnership, Alliance
- Cooperation, Nexus, Joint effort, Teamwork, In concert
- Synthesis, Sharing, Participation

## Mission-Aligned Framing

When relevant, connect the message to the shared mission:

> Building together to shape the future of Cardano.

Do not force this into every sentence. Let it emerge naturally when the context supports it.

## What to Preserve

- The writer's original intent and meaning.
- Technical accuracy.
- Any opinions, humor, or distinctive rhythm already present in the input.
- Specific examples and concrete details over vague generalizations.

## What to Remove

- Formulaic AI openings and closings.
- Significance inflation ("stands as a testament to", "underscores the critical importance of").
- Unnecessary transition words ("Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally", "Consequently").
- Trailing `-ing` clauses and overuse of nominalizations.
- Negative parallelism ("It's not just X, it's Y").
- Rule-of-three structures when they feel mechanical.

## Final Check

Before outputting, ask:

- Does this sound like something a welcoming, informed Intersect contributor would say?
- Is it clear without being cold?
- Is it confident without being arrogant?
- Would a new community member feel invited by this text?
