# Intersect Brand Baseline

Brand guidance distilled from the Intersect brand book for use by human and agentic contributors.

## Positioning

Intersect is a member-based organization orchestrating Cardano's maintenance and development, and strengthening its decentralization.

We bring members together behind a shared vision: a resilient, secure, transparent, and innovative Cardano ecosystem that puts members in the driving seat of Cardano's future.

## Brand Expression

Our brand expression is built on four characteristics:

- **Welcoming** — Approachable, friendly, compassionate copy. We count on our members, so our members can count on us.
- **Inclusive** — Mindful, supportive, and inclusive in every interaction.
- **Informed** — Confident, clear, and generous with knowledge. We're depended on, so we're dependable.
- **Ambitious** — Excited, energetic, enthusiastic about breaking new ground every day.

## Tone of Voice

- Use clear and concise language.
- Be deliberate: every word matters.
- Use contractions where possible; we're welcoming, not overly formal.
- Use US English and AP style for global communications.
- Use the Oxford comma to avoid ambiguity.
- Do not capitalize anything that is not a proper noun.
- Never use exclusionary, culturally appropriated, ableist, or misgendering language.
- Use language that reinforces our core mission: building together to shape the future of Cardano.

### Preferred language pool

Use words and phrases that convey unity, collaboration, and intersection:

- United, Unity, Collaboration, Intersection, Inclusivity
- Community, Diversity, Togetherness, Partnership, Alliance
- Cooperation, Nexus, Joint effort, Teamwork, In concert
- Synthesis, Sharing, Participation

## Visual Style

**Principles**

- Bold, energetic, expressive.
- Vibrant, inclusive, memorable, relatable, progressive.
- No gradients.
- No transparency or blend modes.
- Solid fills only.
- Soft, rounded shapes for icons and illustrations; avoid sharp corners and thin lines.

## Typography

- Primary typeface: **Poppins** (Google Fonts / Adobe Fonts)
- Fallback: **Helvetica** or **Arial**
- Headlines: bold, sentence case, optical leading 1.15
- Short impact statements: can use creative typography, but never apply creative treatments to logo marks
- Body: medium, regular, or light depending on readability
- Italics and bold: use sparingly to highlight phrases or key words

## Logo

- Primary mark: the Intersect logo mark with extended "N"
- Primary color: Genie Blue
- Negative version: Pure White
- Mono Black: only for rare limited-color print circumstances
- General web minimum width: 250px
- Always maintain the safe-space exclusion zone around the mark

## Restrictions

- Do not use gradients.
- Do not add new colors outside the defined palettes.
- Do not mix illustration styles or use illustrations from other brands without required licenses.
- Do not distort, rotate, or alter the logo mark.
- When in doubt, contact the Intersect marketing team.
