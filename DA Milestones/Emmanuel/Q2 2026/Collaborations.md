### Conclusion

Intersect's developer advocacy support — through direct outreach, structured onboarding, and inclusion in working groups and events — has been the enabling factor behind these outcomes. Community members who were introduced to Cardano open source through Intersect-facilitated touchpoints are now contributing to production repositories, leading technical sessions, applying for funded maintainer roles, and engaging in Cardano governance. The results above reflect what happens when Intersect actively invests in regional communities rather than expecting organic discovery.
