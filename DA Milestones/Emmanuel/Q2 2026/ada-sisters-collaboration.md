# ADA Sisters – Involvement & Collaboration with Intersect
**Strategic Partnership Documentation**
**Date:** June 2026




## Executive Summary

ADA Sisters seeks a strategic partnership with Intersect to strengthen its contribution to the Cardano and Midnight communities while expanding opportunities for African women in blockchain development, governance, and Web3 innovation.




## About ADA Sisters

**Mission:** Educating and empowering women across Africa in Cardano and Midnight blockchain technologies — bridging the gap between technical innovation and accessibility.

**Vision:** African women leading the blockchain revolution as builders, contributors, and governance participants, with economic opportunity through blockchain accessible to all.




## Proposed Involvement

### Working Group Participation

ADA Sisters members to attend Intersect Developer Experience Working Group sessions as active community participants — contributing to discussions, sharing community feedback, and staying connected to ecosystem developments.

### Dev Advocate Support at ADA Sisters Events

Intersect developer advocates to attend ADA Sisters-hosted events as:
- **Speakers** — presenting on Cardano development, open source contribution, and governance
- **Hackathon facilitators** — guiding participants through contribution workflows and Intersect repositories

### OSC Meeting Inclusion

ADA Sisters representatives to attend OSC meetings as community members, participating in open source governance discussions on equal footing with other Intersect community participants.




## Objectives

- Onboard African women as active Intersect community members and contributors
- Build bridges between ADA Sisters and Intersect working groups and committees
- Create pathways for women to contribute to Cardano open source projects
- Localize Cardano educational content for African communities
