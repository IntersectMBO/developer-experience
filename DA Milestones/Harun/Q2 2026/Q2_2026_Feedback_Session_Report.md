## Primary Feedback Session: Scalus Project Spotlight

- **Format:** Recorded technical interview, walkthrough, and feedback discussion
- **Facilitator:** Harun Waweru Mwangi
- **Core Contributors:** Alexander Nemish and Oleksii Khodakivskyi
- **Project:** [Scalus](https://scalus.org/)

The session evaluated innovations including Scala-to-UPLC compilation, full-transaction testing, node emulation, cost profiling, IDE debugging, JavaScript integration, and support for contracts loaded through Plutus blueprints. Questions focused on developer fit, performance, security review, interoperability, onboarding, contribution needs, sustainability, and the project roadmap.

The discussion clarified that Scalus is not limited to writing smart contracts in Scala: projects can retain contracts written in languages such as Aiken while using Scalus for transaction construction, testing, emulation, and debugging. It also surfaced opportunities for additional providers, examples, independent auditing support, and a future DevEx session demonstrating how to test an Aiken contract through Scalus.

---

## Supporting Q2 Feedback

- **Session 18 – Using AI in Your Cardano Dev Workflow:** Co-facilitated discussion identified missing MCP support for Cardano on-chain repositories and the need for stronger AI-assisted verification in contributor workflows.
- **Session 21 – Building a Production Cardano SDK:** The live SDK and web-app demonstration invited questions, comments, and suggestions. Feedback surfaced multisig governance, dispute handling, first-recipient default risk, and possible guarantor or shared-loss models.
- **Intersect security-policy rollout:** I opened feedback issues across 15 Intersect repositories asking core maintainers to assess adoption of the organisation-standard `SECURITY.md`. Four of the issues I raised have since been closed: Kupo and Ogmios during Q2, followed by Plutus and `cardano-api` after Q2. These closures show that maintainers reviewed and acted on the proposed standard.
