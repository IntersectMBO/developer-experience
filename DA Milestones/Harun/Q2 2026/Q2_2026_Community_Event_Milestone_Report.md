**Goal:** Attend Web3 events to enrich and improve the developer experience.

**Contract Milestone:** Yes.

## Achievement Summary

I attended two in-person Web3 events in Nairobi during Q2 2026: the two-day Kenya Blockchain & Crypto Conference and a separate VALR networking meetup held alongside the conference. The events provided opportunities to observe practical payment innovations, engage with Web3 and open-source professionals, and introduce Intersect and Cardano to new contacts.

---

## Events Attended

### 1. Kenya Blockchain & Crypto Conference 2026

**Date:** May 14–15, 2026  
**Location:** A.S.K Dome, Nairobi, Kenya  
**Format:** In-Person Conference  
**Website:** [kenyablockchainandcryptoconference.co.ke](https://kenyablockchainandcryptoconference.co.ke/)  
**Program:** [KBCC 2026 Program](https://kenyablockchainandcryptoconference.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Kenya-Blockchain-Crypto-Conference-2026-Program.pdf)

The fourth Kenya Blockchain & Crypto Conference focused on stablecoins, payments, and the next phase of Africa's digital economy. I attended both days, taking part in conference sessions, exhibition activity, and networking with participants from the blockchain, fintech, and open-source communities.

Kotani Pay and Block Co-op attracted significant attention through their exhibition stands. [Taran App](https://taranapp.com/) also drew a large gathering by demonstrating how users can exchange between M-Pesa and stablecoins such as USDC and USDT. The demonstration provided a practical example of blockchain infrastructure connecting directly with payment tools already used in East Africa.

During the conference, I met Sharon Murugi, Communications Lead at Btrust, and introduced one attendee to blockchain and Cardano. The latter engagement will be revisited in the Q2 developer onboarding report as the conversation progresses into practical ecosystem onboarding.

### 2. VALR Nairobi Meetup

**Date:** May 2026, held alongside KBCC  
**Location:** Nairobi Street Kitchen, Nairobi, Kenya  
**Format:** In-Person Industry Networking Meetup  
**Reference:** [VALR's Kenya expansion and event summary](https://blog.valr.com/blog/valr-africas-leading-digital-asset-infrastructure-provider-eyes-kenya-for-expansion)

VALR hosted a separate Nairobi meetup for business and finance leaders during the conference period. I met Peter Mwangi, VALR's Country Manager for Kenya, and introduced him to Intersect, its open-source mission, and the Developer Advocate program.

I also met Chizaram Ucheaga, co-founder and COO of [Timon](https://www.usetimon.com/), a travel-focused financial platform using stablecoin infrastructure for cross-border payments. These conversations extended the quarter's engagement beyond conference attendance by creating awareness of Intersect among regional fintech and Web3 leaders.

## Key Themes

Stablecoins and their connection to existing African payment systems were the clearest themes across both events. The interest around Taran App and other payment providers showed that practical on-ramps, off-ramps, and mobile-money integration are central to regional adoption.

The events also created opportunities to introduce Intersect and Cardano outside the existing ecosystem. Engagement with attendees, open-source professionals, and fintech leaders established potential relationships that can be developed through later onboarding and community work.

---

## Conclusion

Attendance at KBCC 2026 and the VALR Nairobi meetup fulfilled the Q2 community engagement requirement. Together, the events provided direct exposure to regional Web3 payment innovation and opportunities to represent Intersect, introduce Cardano, and build relationships across Nairobi's blockchain and fintech community.
