## Project Overview

The DCU Toolkit uses Cardano smart contracts to enforce the rules of rotating savings and credit associations. The session connected three layers: Aiken validators, a typed TypeScript SDK built with Lucid Evolution and Effect, and a demo web app (Kyama).

The walkthrough covered blueprint-derived identifiers, aligned on-chain and off-chain schemas, typed errors, and endpoint testing. The live demonstration used the CLI and Kyama on Cardano Preprod to create an account and savings circle, connect Lace wallets, and join the circle from a second wallet.
