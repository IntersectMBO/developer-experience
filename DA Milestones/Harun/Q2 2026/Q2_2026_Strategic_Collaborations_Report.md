## Overview

Three strategic collaboration efforts were progressed during Q2 2026. Outreach to Scalus resulted in a completed Project Spotlight, outreach to Hydrozoa established a future opportunity when the team has capacity, and an introduction between Tx Meta and Eburnie Labs progressed toward a potential training and distribution agreement.

---

## Strategic Collaboration Log

### 1. Scalus Project Spotlight

**Contacted:** Alexander Nemish and Oleksii Khodakivskyi, Scalus founders and core contributors  
**Purpose:** Invite the Scalus team to present its open-source Cardano development platform through the Project Spotlight series.  
**Response and Outcome:** The team accepted the invitation and participated in a recorded technical spotlight. The session introduced Scalus to the wider developer community and explored its Scala and JVM workflow, transaction testing, emulation, profiling, debugging, interoperability, and roadmap.  
**Next Steps:** Maintain the connection through DevEx and invite the team to a future Working Group session demonstrating how Scalus can test contracts written in Aiken.

### 2. Hydrozoa Project Spotlight Outreach

**Contacted:** Ilia Rodionov, Hydrozoa contributor  
**Purpose:** Invite the Hydrozoa team to present its lightweight Cardano state-channel protocol through the Project Spotlight series.  
**Response and Outcome:** Ilia responded that the team could not commit to a session because of current time constraints, but would reconnect when the team has capacity to consider one.  
**Next Steps:** Leave the invitation open and follow up when the Hydrozoa team indicates that it is ready to participate.

### 3. Tx Meta and Eburnie Labs

**Contacted:** [Cyprien N'GUESSAN](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cyprien-n-guessan-77a9501/) of Eburnie Labs, introduced to Tx Meta  
**Purpose:** Explore collaboration on distributing and delivering blockchain-on-Cardano training using the open-source DCU Toolkit and its Kyama demonstration application.  
**Response and Outcome:** Both organisations expressed interest and are working toward an agreement covering distribution and training. The agreement had not been signed by the end of the reporting period.  
**Next Steps:** Finalise the agreement, define the training scope and responsibilities, and establish a delivery and distribution plan for the DCU Toolkit and Kyama learning material.
